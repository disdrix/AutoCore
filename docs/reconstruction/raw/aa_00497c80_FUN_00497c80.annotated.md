## Machine-level notes

- **ABI:** `56 8B F1` → ESI=ECX=this; bare `C3`; `B0 01` → AL=1.
- **Count @+0x10:** loop decrements until `< 1`; ends at 0 when entries drained.
- **Bank @+0x14:** 0xA0 bytes / 40 dwords of entry pointers; nulls skipped; non-null get `FUN_00497900` (vector-dtor of 0x28 × 0xD0 elems at entry+0x20) then `operator_delete`.
- **Decompiler bug:** shows `break` + non-returning `operator_delete`. Bytes: after delete, `cmp [esi+0x10],0 / jg loop` — **loop continues**.
- **FUN_007874b0:** thiscall ECX=`this+0xB8`, args (0,0) — frees 2D buffer if present, sets dims 0×0 (see ctor twin `FUN_00787520` which only zeroed the triple).
- **FUN_00788db0(this+0x14, 0xA0):** memset-style zero of entire bank after free.
- **Fields after clear:** `+4=0`, `+8=0`, `+0xC=0`, `*this=1` (flag). Ctor had `+0xC=5` capacity; clear zeros it (callers may re-set, e.g. `FUN_004982d0` writes 10).
- **Related ctor:** `ListTrackedCtx_Ctor_Inferred` (`0x00497920`, W32-P).
