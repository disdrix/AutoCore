## Machine-level notes

- SEH-split **worker fragment** of a larger OLE DB accessor setup spanning `FUN_00421240` → this → `FUN_0042139b` → `FUN_00421677`.
- Pre-entry `0x0042135f`: `mov ebx,[ebp+8]` loads **ctx*** into EBX (shared with sibling build).
- Body does **not** own a portable prologue/epilogue; fail jumps to shared cleanup; success **jmps** into `FUN_0042139b`.
- Decompiler may surface `FUN_0042139b()` as a call; bytes are **`EB 09` jmp** (tail-join).
