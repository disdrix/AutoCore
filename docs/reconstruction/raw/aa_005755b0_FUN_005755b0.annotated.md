### Decompiler traps

1. `FUN_00520330(param_1)` with host-only — **false**. Bytes: `push host; mov ecx, member; call` → writes **`member+0xCB0`**.
2. `FUN_00418d70` this is **`host+0x1F4`**, not the host root.
3. Free test is bitwise AND of two dwords against `0xFFFFFFFF` (requires both `0xFFFFFFFF`).
