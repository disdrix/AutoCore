## Machine-level notes

- **ESI = list\*** at entry. No `MOV ESI,ECX` prologue (contrast ECX twin `0x00404060`).
- Layout: head\* @ `list+4`, size @ `list+8` (MSVC `std::list` shell).
- Sentinel node: `_Next` @ +0, `_Prev` @ +4; re-ring both to self.
- Free loop walks former `_Next` chain with `operator_delete` until back at head. Decompiler false-noreturn on delete **truncates** loop in pseudocode; **bytes seal full loop**.
- Does **not** free sentinel; parents often `operator_delete(*(list+4))` after clear (e.g. dialog @+0x4e0).
- Primary parent for this dual: `CNDUIDialog_CompleteDtor` (`0x00792c20`) with `ESI = this+0x4dc`.

  return;
}
