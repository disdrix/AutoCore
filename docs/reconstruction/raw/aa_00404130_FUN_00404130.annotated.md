## Machine-level notes

- **Role:** MSVC-style `uninitialized_fill_n` / insert hole fill for elements of stride **`0x98`** (UI toast POD).
- **Single callee:** `FUN_00404670` = sealed `UiToast_ConstructN_0x98` (loop of `UiToast_PodCopyElement_0x98` / `FUN_00404710`, 0x26 dwords each).
- **Return:** end pointer `dst + count * 0x98` via lea/shift chain (not imul).
- **ABI:** `ret 0x0C` — three stack formals `(dst, count, src)`. Ghidra labels a thiscall `param_1` (ECX); ECX is **only** pushed as a phantom formal into ConstructN and is **not** part of the result. ConstructN is cdecl with used formals `(dst,count,src)`; this wrapper pushes **five** dwords and cleans `0x14` after the call (documented phantom trailing pushes; same family as ConstructN dual notes).
- **Twin:** `CombatFloater_UninitializedFillN_0x38` @ `0x00402ea0` (same ConstructN+end pattern, stride 0x38).
- **Not** capacity growth, relocate, or assign-over-range — pure fill + end.
