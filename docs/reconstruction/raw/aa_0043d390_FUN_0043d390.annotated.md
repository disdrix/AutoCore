- **Shared leaf** ring-aware **dword** copy used by assign (`FUN_0043ce90`), erase forward (`FUN_0043c8c0`), and related helpers (`FUN_0043cb00`, `FUN_0043cf40`).
- Not exclusive to one parent — do not treat as private to RingCopyRange alone.
